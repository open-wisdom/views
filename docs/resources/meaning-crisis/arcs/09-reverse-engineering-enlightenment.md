# 09: Reverse Engineering Enlightenment (Episodes 36–39)

## Perennial Problems, Ecologies of Practice, Agape, 4E Cognition, Religion of No Religion — From Diagnosis to Practice

This arc pivots from theoretical analysis to pragmatic response. Having established the cognitive science of relevance realization (Arc 7) and the ontology of sacredness (Arc 8), Vervaeke now asks: given this understanding, what do we *do*? The answer unfolds across four episodes that map the structural vulnerabilities of relevance realization (perennial problems), match specific practices to specific problems, ground the entire project in 4E cognitive science and agape, and propose the institutional form of a "religion that is not a religion."

**Perennial problems: the structural vulnerabilities of relevance realization.** [Episode 36](../episodes/summary-36-religio-perennial-problems-reverse-engineering-enlightenment.md) reframes enlightenment from mystical achievement to functional process: "any reliable developmental transformation of perspectival, participatory, and procedural knowing that ameliorates perennial problems." This is a precise, scientifically tractable definition. The meaning crisis has two converging sources: *historical* (the genealogical collapse traced in Arcs 2–6 eliminated the worldviews and psychotechnologies that cultures developed) and *structural* (independent of history, the relevance realization machinery has inherent vulnerabilities that affect all humans across all cultures). This convergence explains both why our current situation is unprecedented and why traditional practices remain relevant — they addressed problems that persist regardless of cultural context.

The perennial problems map across three levels. At the *functional level* (how RR operates): parasitic processing (self-deception that becomes adaptively self-perpetuating) and modal confusion (confusing Having mode with Being mode). At the *structural level* (the meta-meaning system): the reflectiveness gap (trade-off between immersion and paralyzing reflection), absurdity (not from logical argument but from perspectival clash — what matters in one frame becomes meaningless in another), and anxiety (pervasive threat without object, arising from inner conflict). At the *developmental level* (how we change): alienation (disconnection from others), existential inertia (inability to move between worldviews), existential ignorance (inability to infer knowledge one isn't currently cultivating), and existential entrapment (convergence of inertia and ignorance).

A crucial distinction: *psycho-cultural indispensability* versus *metaphysical necessity*. A symbol, practice, or framework may be necessary for a person given their history and cognitive makeup (indispensable) without being eternally necessary for all beings (metaphysically necessary). This protects the value of particular traditions while rejecting fundamentalism — you may genuinely need Christianity or Buddhism without Christianity or Buddhism being the only path.

**The ecology of psychotechnologies.** [Episode 37](../episodes/summary-37-reverse-engineering-enlightenment-part-2.md) delivers the practical response by matching specific practices to specific perennial problems. Counteractive dynamical systems (the Eightfold Path model) address parasitic processing. Sati (remembrance of the Being mode) addresses modal confusion. Flow states (the yin-yang integration of being challenged at the edge of competence) address the reflectiveness gap. Scientia intuitiva (Spinoza) and prajna (Buddhism) — perspectives interpenetrating simultaneously — address absurdity. Inner dialogue (internalizing the sage, the Stoic "internal Socrates") addresses anxiety. Communitas (collective flow) and authentic discourse address alienation. Gnosis (higher states of consciousness within sapiential community) addresses existential entrapment.

The key structural concept is *counteractive dynamical system*: not a one-shot intervention but an internalized, self-organizing ecology of mutually supporting practices that can adapt as parasitic processing reconfigures itself. Isolated practices get outflanked by the self-organizing dynamics of self-deception; only a system of practices that is itself self-organizing can keep pace. Central to all of these is *wisdom framing* — the overarching cognitive style where amelioration of self-deception and affordance of self-optimization are paramount.

**Agape and 4E cognitive science: worldview legitimation.** [Episode 38](../episodes/summary-38-agape-and-4e-cognitive-science.md) addresses a critical gap: even effective practices remain undermined without worldview legitimation — a framework that makes it rational to engage in them. Two problems must be solved. First, Susan Wolf's problem of objective attractiveness: meaning requires subjective attraction to meet objective value, but scientific materialism denies that value exists objectively. Vervaeke's response: reframe from "are things objectively valuable?" to "what conditions afford meaning-making itself?" *Agape* is reconceived as caring about the conditions that make caring possible — neither subjective (arbitrary) nor objective (substance-like property) but *transjective*: emerging in the agent-arena relationship. This is transcendental in the Kantian sense: it addresses the conditions for the possibility of meaning itself.

Second, the loss of the three orders of meaning (nomological, normative, narrative) through the scientific worldview must be addressed. *4E cognitive science* — Embodied, Embedded, Enactive, Extended — restores deep continuity: mind arises through immediate coping (not symbol manipulation), is continuous with environment through niche construction, is something enacted (not passively received), and extends beyond the brain through tools and distributed cognition. There is no ontological break between physics, biology, cognition, and consciousness — only increasing complexification. This rejects Cartesian dualism and establishes that we *belong to* nature rather than observe it from outside.

Vervaeke proposes *transcendence into* the depths of nature and psyche (following Goodenough) rather than transcendence above — moving from horizontal narrative identity (cosmic purpose) to vertical depth (present participatory connection). This opens the possibility of post-narrative ways of being grounded in higher states of consciousness rather than historical storylines.

**The religion of no religion.** [Episode 39](../episodes/summary-39-the-religion-of-no-religion.md) addresses the structural question: how do we cultivate these practices at scale? The central distinction is between *religio* (lived transformative practice) and *credo* (propositional belief system). Both have functional roles, but *credal dominance* — where right beliefs become more important than transformation — is pathological. Signal detection theory illuminates this: all cognition requires setting decision criteria to distinguish signal from noise, and creeds serve this function for spiritual practice. But optimal criteria are contextually sensitive, not absolute. Creeds must be flexibly adjusted, never fixed absolutely.

The proposal is an *ecology of psychotechnologies* operating bidirectionally: top-down (from propositional frameworks to participatory knowing) and bottom-up (allowing participatory emergence to inform conscious understanding). This requires *meta-psychotechnologies* — second-order capacity for deliberately engineering and coordinating practices into self-correcting systems. Collectively, this parallels wisdom as the individual meta-virtue.

The arc concludes with the deepest definition of wisdom in the series: *seeing through illusion into reality* — systematic insight that addresses multiple misframed problems simultaneously by intervening at the ontological level of framing itself. This is relevance realization operating on itself: the capacity to recognize when the very frameworks through which we perceive reality are generating distortion, and to transform those frameworks rather than merely adjusting beliefs within them.

**The ontological trajectory.** This arc completes the constructive project that began in Arc 7. Where that arc built the cognitive science of relevance realization, Arc 8 showed that sacredness is its phenomenological disclosure, and this arc provides the practical framework for engaging it. The ontological commitments are now fully explicit: meaning is participatory, not propositional; transformation requires ecologies of practice, not belief-adoption; wisdom is meta-cognitive intervention in framing processes, not accumulation of correct judgments; and the institutional form this takes must be a "religion of no religion" — systematic meaning-making practice grounded in neither supernatural metaphysics nor political ideology.

What gets carried forward is the practical architecture. The next arcs will develop the theory of wisdom (what kind of rationality, intelligence, and virtue does it require?) and examine contemporary thinkers who have proposed alternative ontologies for addressing the meaning crisis. But the core proposal is already in place: an evidence-based ecology of psychotechnologies, grounded in the cognitive science of relevance realization, organized around the amelioration of perennial problems, legitimated by 4E cognition and agape, and institutionalized as religio rather than credo.
