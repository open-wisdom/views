## Notes on Using This Index

**For linear reading**: Follow the Chronological Arc sequence (Layer 2A) and use this index to trace how concepts introduced in early episodes are developed and transformed later.

**For thematic research**: Use Part 2 to find all concepts relevant to a particular ontological question (e.g., all concepts related to how meaning breaks down, or all concepts related to practices of transformation).

**For the Open Wisdom project**: The groupings in Part 2 map onto the project's core concerns: Group A (foundations) informs the theoretical framework, Group B (cognitive science) provides the scientific grounding, Group E (degenerative dynamics) diagnoses what needs to change, Group G (practices) suggests methods, and Group H (wisdom) articulates the goal.

**Cross-group connections**: Many concepts span groups. Relevance Realization (Group B) grounds the Sacred (Group D) and enables Wisdom (Group H). Parasitic Processing (Group E) is the inverse of Anagoge (Group C). The historical shifts (Group F) systematically removed the conditions identified in Group A. These connections are mapped in detail in the L3-02 Concept Dependency Map.
