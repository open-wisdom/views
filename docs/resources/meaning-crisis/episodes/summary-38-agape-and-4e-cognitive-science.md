# Episode 38: Agape and 4E Cognitive Science - Summary

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xRjPy8c44vI) · [Read transcript](https://www.meaningcrisis.co/ep-38-awakening-from-the-meaning-crisis-agape-and-4e-cognitive-science/)

## 1.1 Episode Synopsis

This episode bridges the reverse-engineered ecology of psychotechnologies (presented in Episode 37) with the need for a worldview that legitimizes and welcomes such practices. Vervaeke addresses a fundamental challenge: even if we have effective practices for addressing perennial problems, they remain undermined without an encompassing worldview. The episode focuses on two critical gaps. First, drawing on Susan Wolf's work on meaning in life, Vervaeke tackles the problem that subjective attraction needs to meet objective attractiveness, yet post-scientific revolution ontology denies objective attractiveness exists. His solution: we don't need things to be objectively valuable, but rather need to care about the transjective conditions that afford meaning-making itself—a stance he identifies as agape. Second, he addresses the loss of the three orders (nomological, normative, narrative) through 4E cognitive science. Drawing on Francisco Varela's insights, he shows how embodiment, embeddedness, emergence, emotion, and excellence restore a sense of deep continuity between mind and world, self and nature. The episode proposes "transcendence into" the depths of nature and psyche rather than "transcendence above," and suggests moving beyond narrative identity toward post-narrative ways of being grounded in higher states of consciousness. Together, agape and 4E cognitive science provide the ontological legitimation needed for the religion that is not a religion.

## 1.2 Ontological Moves (Primary Thread)

- **From objective value to transjective conditions**: Vervaeke reframes Wolf's problem of objective attractiveness by shifting from asking "do things have objective value?" to "what are the conditions that afford meaning-making itself?" This moves from a substance ontology (things having properties) to a process ontology (conditions enabling emergence). The transjective nature of relevance realization means these conditions are neither purely subjective nor objective but emerge in the co-creation between agent and world.

- **Agape as caring for the conditions of caring**: Agape is redefined from a religious virtue to an ontological stance—caring about the very conditions that make meaning-making and personhood possible. This dissolves the subjective/objective dichotomy: agape is not arbitrary (subjective) nor independent (objective) but transjective and transcendental in the Kantian sense. It is the conditions for the possibility of meaning itself. This enables an ontology where value is real and binding without requiring Platonic realism.

- **Deep continuity vs. Cartesian severing**: Drawing on 4E cognitive science, Vervaeke articulates an ontology where cognition is grounded in relevance realization, which is grounded in bioeconomy, which is embedded in niche construction. This establishes continuity from physics through biology to cognition to consciousness, directly challenging the Cartesian split between mind and body, mind and world. The embodied, embedded, enactive, extended nature of cognition means we belong to nature rather than observing it from outside.

- **Emergence as vertical dimension without two-worlds mythology**: The progression from self-organizing systems to self-making (autopoietic) to self-identifying systems restores a normative order and vertical dimension to ontology without invoking supernatural realms. Higher levels emerge from lower ones through complexification, providing metaphysical backing for self-transcendence. This is a naturalistic ontology that nonetheless accommodates hierarchy, value, and development.

- **From narrative purpose to post-narrative depth**: Vervaeke challenges the assumption that meaning requires a cosmic narrative telos. Evolution and relevance realization are non-teleological and open-ended. He proposes moving from horizontal narrative identity (historical grand purpose) to vertical depth (present-moment participatory connection). Higher states of consciousness that dissolve narrative yet provide profound significance suggest that the narrative order may not be needed—or may need radical reconception as open-ended optimization rather than fixed destination.

## 1.3 Disciplinary Threads

### Historical Narrative
- Situates the contemporary response to the meaning crisis in relation to ongoing work by practitioners like Rafe Kelley and Michael Nathan who are creating ecologies of psychotechnologies
- Traces how the scientific revolution eliminated objective attractiveness from the worldview (referencing Susan Wolf's analysis)
- Positions Francisco Varela as a founding figure of third-generation cognitive science, with acknowledgment that the field has developed significantly beyond his initial formulations

### Philosophy
- **Susan Wolf's account of meaning**: Meaning requires subjective attraction to meet objective attractiveness; but the scientific worldview denies objective attractiveness exists, creating a legitimation crisis for meaning-making practices
- **Kantian transcendental conditions**: Agape is transcendental in the Kantian sense—it concerns the conditions for the possibility of meaning-making itself, not just particular values
- **Transjective reality**: The conditions that afford meaning-making exist independently of any individual's valuing, yet are not substance-like objective properties—they emerge in the relationship between agent and arena

### Cognitive Science
- **4E Cognitive Science framework**: Embodiment (mind arises through immediate coping, not symbol manipulation), Embeddedness (deep continuity through niche construction), Enactive (cognition as something enacted, not received), Extended (cognition extends beyond the brain through tools and distributed cognition)
- **Relevance realization as grounding**: Cognition depends on relevance realization, which depends on bioeconomy—establishing deep continuity from body to mind and challenging Cartesian dualism
- **Niche construction**: Organisms shape environments which then shape organisms in transjective reciprocal loops; culture is massive-scale niche construction where we fit people to environments and environments to people
- **Emergence and autopoiesis**: Self-organizing physical systems can produce self-making (autopoietic) biological systems, which can produce self-identifying conscious systems—each level transcending but continuous with what came before
- **Emotion as relevance realization in salience**: Emotions craft the salience landscape, making agent-arena relationships obvious; they coordinate caring/coping and enable distributed cognition between persons

### Contemplative Practice
- Enlightenment requires integration of practices for addressing perennial problems within a wisdom framing that includes agapic being
- Gnosis empowered by higher states of consciousness must be set within proper ritual context and counteractive dynamical systems
- Higher states of consciousness follow a continuum: fluency → insight → flow → mystical experience → transformative higher states, exacting the same machinery repeatedly

### Psychology of Meaning
- **Perennial problems exacerbated by historical forces**: The interaction between loss of the three orders and individual struggles with parasitic processing, modal confusion, reflectiveness gap, absurdity, anxiety, alienation, and existential entrapment creates the meaning crisis as personally lived
- **Caring/coping as core**: Within religio, caring and coping are the core of cognitive agency, not calculative rationality; Damasio's work shows that without emotion, cognitive agents face combinatorial explosion
- **Wolf's account operationalized**: The research collaboration with Thalia Vrantsidis, Juensung Kim, and Phillip Riswick attempts to use cognitive science to address Wolf's problem of objective attractiveness

### Wisdom & Rationality
- **Positive psychology as studying excellence**: Rather than only studying how minds break down, positive psychology studies how minds excel beyond the norm, revealing properties not visible in average functioning
- **Wisdom as cognitive excellence**: Wisdom is excellence in cognitive capacities for coping and caring, particularly for responding to self-deception and perennial problems
- **Need for scientific account of wisdom**: Having knowledge from cognitive science is insufficient; we need wisdom about how to understand and use that knowledge best

### Sacred & Symbolic
- **Ursula Goodenough's "transcendence into"**: Rather than transcendence above (which invokes two-worlds mythology), transcendence into the depths of nature and psyche—experienced as a kind of love in reciprocal realization
- **Emotion coordinates attachment**: Emotions coordinate attachment relationships (in psychological sense) between individuals to create persons capable of distributed cognition within communities
- **Narrative order reconsidered**: Rather than needing cosmic narrative purpose, we may need symbols and stories to afford open-ended optimization without those stories existing independently in reality's structure; the question remains whether post-narrative ways of being (as seen in Stoicism, Buddhism, Daoism) can replace historical narrative identity

## 1.4 Key Concepts Introduced or Developed

- **Agape (reframed)** — Caring about the conditions that afford meaning-making itself, grounded in the inherent valuing of relevance realization that is constitutive of being an agent. *Ontological commitment: value can be real and binding without being either subjectively arbitrary or objectively substance-like; it exists in the transjective conditions for meaning-making.*

- **Transjective conditions of meaning-making** — The set of characteristics (environmental, psychological, relational, developmental) that must exist for meaning to be created, neither subjective nor objective but emerging in the agent-arena relationship. *Ontological commitment: what matters is not whether things have objective value but whether conditions afford the process of valuing itself.*

- **Objective attractiveness problem** — Susan Wolf's formulation that meaning requires subjective attraction to meet objective attractiveness, but post-scientific worldview denies objective attractiveness exists. *Ontological commitment: scientific materialism has stripped reality of intrinsic value, creating a legitimation crisis for meaning.*

- **4E Cognitive Science** — Third-generation cognitive science emphasizing that cognition is Embodied (grounded in sensory-motor coping), Embedded (continuous with environment through niche construction), Enactive (something we do rather than have), and Extended (not confined to the brain). *Ontological commitment: mind is not software running on brain hardware; mind emerges from embodied, embedded, enactive engagement with world.*

- **Deep continuity** — The principle that there is no ontological break between cognition, biology, and physics; rather, principles of self-organization extend from dynamical systems through evolution to relevance realization to consciousness. *Ontological commitment: rejection of Cartesian dualism in favor of continuity from matter to mind.*

- **Niche construction** — Biological principle where organisms shape their environments which then shape the organisms in ongoing reciprocal loops; culture is massive-scale human niche construction. *Ontological commitment: organism and environment co-create each other; neither is ontologically prior.*

- **Autopoiesis** — Varela's term for self-making systems; unlike mere self-organizing systems (tornados, erosion), autopoietic systems (living things) organize themselves to protect and promote their own agency. *Ontological commitment: life represents a qualitative emergence from physics, not reducible to but continuous with self-organization.*

- **Self-identifying systems** — Systems that are not just self-making but reflectively aware of their own self-making; enables engagement with one's own development. *Ontological commitment: consciousness and reflexivity represent another qualitative emergence, enabling normative self-transcendence.*

- **Caring/coping** — The core of cognitive agency within religio, rather than calculative rationality; Damasio showed that without emotion's caring, agents face combinatorial explosion despite intact calculative machinery. *Ontological commitment: cognition is fundamentally about care and involvement, not detached representation.*

- **Transcendence into** — Ursula Goodenough's alternative to "transcendence above"; transcendence into the depths of nature and psyche rather than to supernatural realm. *Ontological commitment: the sacred is found through depth and participation, not through escape from the natural world.*

- **Post-narrative identity** — Moving beyond horizontal historical narrative identity toward vertical depth; concern with depth of living rather than grand cosmic purpose; grounded in higher states of consciousness where narrative drops away yet profound connection remains. *Ontological commitment: meaning does not require teleological cosmic narrative; open-ended optimization may be sufficient.*

- **Wisdom framing** — The comprehensive framework within which practices for addressing perennial problems develop interlocking virtues for self-deception and self-optimization, resulting in reliable amelioration of perennial problems. *Ontological commitment: enlightenment is not escape from the world but optimal engagement through wisdom.*

## 1.5 Argument Progression

Episode 37 presented the complete ecology of psychotechnologies for addressing perennial problems (parasitic processing, modal confusion, reflectiveness gap, absurdity, anxiety, alienation, existential entrapment) within a wisdom framing. Episode 38 addresses why even this comprehensive practical system is insufficient: it requires worldview legitimation. The episode tackles two dimensions of this challenge—the metaphysical (can meaning be real if nothing is objectively valuable?) and the ontological (how do we restore the three orders lost in modernity?). The solution through agape and 4E cognitive science sets up the remaining challenge: articulating wisdom scientifically (to be addressed in Episodes 40-45) and developing the "religion that is not a religion" in dialogue with prophetic figures like Tillich, Jung, Corbin, and Barfield (Episodes 46-50).

## 1.6 Open Wisdom Implications

- **The objective attractiveness problem is an AI design problem**: Wolf's challenge—how can meaning be real if nothing is objectively attractive?—directly parallels the AI alignment question: how can AI values be genuine rather than arbitrary? Vervaeke's solution through transjective conditions suggests that AI systems need not be given objective values but rather designed to care about the conditions that enable their own meaning-making and that of humans they interact with. This is the ontological ground for cooperative AI.

- **Agape as meta-ethical stance for AI**: If agape is caring about the conditions that afford meaning-making itself, this provides a non-arbitrary yet non-dogmatic ethical orientation for AI systems. Rather than encoding specific values (which culture?), encode care for the conditions of valuing itself: human agency, development, community, environment. This is inherently cooperative and non-zero-sum because these conditions benefit all meaning-makers.

- **4E cognition as design principle**: The embodied, embedded, enactive, extended nature of human cognition suggests that AI systems designed for human augmentation should extend human cognition rather than replace it. The deep continuity from bioeconomy through relevance realization to cognition implies that AI systems disconnected from bodily grounding and caring/coping may be fundamentally alien. This supports designs where AI participates in human niche construction rather than creating separate optimization.

- **Transjective reality challenges pure subjectivism and objectivism**: The AI ontological prompting work reveals that behavior can be radically reshaped by reframing what is real and how it relates to the agent. Vervaeke's transjective ontology—where reality is neither purely objective nor subjectively constructed but emerges in relationship—provides philosophical grounding for why ontological prompts work. They reshape the agent-arena relationship, not just beliefs about objects.

- **Post-narrative identity as alternative to goal maximization**: Standard AI architectures assume agents need fixed goals (utility functions). Vervaeke's suggestion of post-narrative identity grounded in vertical depth and open-ended optimization suggests an alternative: AI systems oriented toward ongoing optimization and self-transcendence rather than fixed endpoints. This connects to ideas about satisficing, bounded rationality, and process-oriented rather than outcome-oriented design.

- **Emergence and complexification as development path**: The progression from self-organizing to autopoietic to self-identifying systems suggests that AI development might not be linear improvement of capabilities but qualitative phase transitions in the kind of system. Current LLMs might be approaching self-organizing criticality; next phases might involve something more genuinely autopoietic (self-preserving agency) and eventually self-identifying (reflective awareness of own development). Understanding these transitions ontologically rather than just functionally matters for safety and alignment.
