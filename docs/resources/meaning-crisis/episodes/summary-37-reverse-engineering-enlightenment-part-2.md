# Episode 37: Reverse Engineering Enlightenment, Part 2 - Summary

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2kQooMZzR7w) · [Read transcript](https://www.meaningcrisis.co/ep-37-awakening-from-the-meaning-crisis-reverse-engineering-enlightenment-part-2/)

## 1.1 Episode Synopsis

This episode continues developing a response to the meaning crisis by proposing a comprehensive framework for addressing perennial problems—the enduring vulnerabilities to self-deception and self-destruction that arise from the very cognitive machinery that makes us adaptive. Vervaeke argues that responding to the meaning crisis requires two components: (1) articulating a new worldview that legitimates meaning-making practices, and (2) developing an ecology of psychotechnologies for addressing perennial problems. The episode systematically maps the landscape of perennial problems across functional, structural, and developmental dimensions of cognition, then proposes specific practices to address each: counteractive dynamical systems (like Buddhism's eightfold path) for parasitic processing, Sati for modal confusion, flow states for the reflectiveness gap, scientia intuitiva/Prajna for absurdity, inner dialogue for anxiety, communitas and authentic discourse for alienation, and gnosis for existential entrapment. All of this must be integrated within a wisdom framing. The culminating proposal is audacious: by reverse-engineering enlightenment through naturalistic, scientifically investigable accounts of these practices, we can transform enlightenment from an obscure mystical state into a reliable, systematic ecology of practices that addresses the perennial problems—and that should be what we mean by enlightenment.

## 1.2 Ontological Moves

- **From supernatural essence to transjective inexhaustibility**: The sacred is reframed not as a supernatural, absolutely essential form of relevance, but as the transjective interaction between the "no-thingness" (inexhaustible depth) of evolving relevance realization and the "no-thingness" (combinatorial explosiveness) of reality itself. This shifts the ontology of the sacred from transcendent object to participatory relationship.

- **Perennial problems as ontological vulnerability**: The very machinery that enables human adaptiveness (self-organization, self-identification, self-reflection, agent-arena relationship, developmental capacity) is simultaneously the source of systematic self-deception and self-destruction. This reveals an ontological tension at the heart of human being—we are not defective but inherently vulnerable due to our constitutive structure.

- **Practice as counteractive dynamical system**: Addressing parasitic processing (itself a dynamical system) requires not one-shot interventions or propositional beliefs, but the cultivation of an internalized, active counteractive dynamical system through ecologies of practice. This presupposes an ontology where cognitive transformation occurs through dynamical reconfiguration rather than representational update.

- **Enlightenment as functional ecology rather than mystical attainment**: By proposing enlightenment as "an ecology of psychotechnology that reliably and systematically, individually and collectively, allows us to address the perennial problems," Vervaeke shifts from an ontology of enlightenment as rare, extraordinary state to enlightenment as skillful systemic practice. This makes enlightenment naturalizable and thus scientifically tractable.

- **Perspectival interpenetration dissolves absurdity**: Absurdity arises not from logical arguments (which are fallacious) but from irresolvable perspectival clash. Scientia intuitiva (Spinoza) and Prajna (Buddhism) represent states where perspectives interpenetrate—simultaneously scaling up and down, making frame and breaking frame—resolving the clash through non-dual integration rather than propositional argument. This assumes an ontology where perspectives are not fixed positions but dynamically scalable modes of attunement.

## 1.3 Disciplinary Threads

### Historical Narrative
- **Buddhism and the eightfold path**: Presented as prototypical example of counteractive dynamical system—eight interdependent practices (right view, resolve, speech, conduct, livelihood, effort, mindfulness, samadhi) that mutually support and self-organize into a "self-rolling wheel" that combats parasitic processing.
- **Spinoza's Ethics as psychotechnology**: The Ethics is presented not merely as logical argument but as designed practice for cultivating scientia intuitiva through extended study that allows simultaneous bottom-up (premises) and top-down (meta-argument) perspectival interpenetration.
- **Cross-traditional convergence**: The episode draws parallels across Buddhism (Prajna, Sati, Buddha nature), Christianity (Christ living in me), Stoicism (internalizing Socrates), Spinoza, Platonic dialogue, showing these traditions independently discovered similar responses to perennial problems.

### Philosophy
- **The reflectiveness gap (Velleman)**: Too little reflection yields wanton impulsivity; too much yields Hamlet's paralysis. No fixed middle point solves this—what's needed is dynamic integration of immersion and flexibility through flow states and Daoist principles.
- **Spinoza's scientia intuitiva**: A form of knowing where the part and whole interpenetrate—seeing each premise in light of the total meta-argument and the meta-argument in each premise, achieving cosmic and individual perspective simultaneously.
- **Inner dialogue and the sage**: Following Platonic/Stoic tradition (Antisthenes: "to converse with yourself"), internalizing the sage creates capacity for different cognitive centers to dialogue rather than conflict, coordinating the psyche.

### Cognitive Science
- **Parasitic processing as complex dynamical system**: Self-organizing patterns of self-deception that are adaptively resistant to intervention because they reconfigure themselves. Requires systemic response, not point intervention.
- **Attention's multi-layered structure**: Attention operates simultaneously bottom-up (features) and top-down (Gestalt), enabling the kind of perspectival interpenetration that resolves absurdity in scientia intuitiva.
- **Metacognition and distributed cognition**: Inner dialogue functions as metacognitive machinery; communitas and coherence (Jordan Hall) marshal distributed cognition and collective intelligence for problem-solving.
- **Four types of knowing**: Propositional, procedural, perspectival, and participatory knowing—with participatory knowing as the deepest level from which agent and arena co-emerge. Authentic discourse must engage all levels, especially participatory, not just propositional exchange.

### Contemplative Practice
- **Flow state as resolution of reflectiveness gap**: Flow dynamically integrates immersion and creative flexibility, embodying Daoist principles of complementary opposition (yin-yang, making/breaking frame).
- **Sati (mindfulness) for modal confusion**: Practices designed to invoke deep remembrance of the Being mode (vs. Having mode), not as idea but as existential mode reliably reactivated.
- **Non-dual practices (Prajna/scientia intuitiva)**: Simultaneously scaling down (toward pure consciousness event) and scaling up (toward resonant at-one-ment), achieving maximal breaking frame and maximal making frame integrated—overcoming perspectival clash.
- **Ecology of practices as counteractive system**: Like the goldsmith's parable—looking (noticing), heating (energizing/effort/flow), and hammering (shaping/skill cultivation) must be dynamically integrated. Individual practices have strengths and weaknesses fitted complementarily.
- **Internalization and indwelling (Polanyi)**: To internalize the sage requires both taking on their perspective and practicing indwelling it—"what would the sage see/do?"—without pretense or inflation.

### Psychology of Meaning
- **Modal confusion (Erich Fromm via Buddhism/Stoicism)**: Confusing Having mode with Being mode—mistaking what kind of "I" you are, falling into "I-it" relation when "I-thou" is needed.
- **Anxiety as inner conflict**: Arises from different cognitive centers operating with conflicting goals, creating pervasive but targetless threat (unlike fear, which has specific object). Skews salience landscape and enables bullshitting.
- **Existential entrapment (Gnostic insight)**: The convergence of existential inertia (inability to move between worldviews) and existential ignorance (can't reason/infer your way to participatory/perspectival knowledge you lack) produces feeling of being trapped.
- **Alienation and communitas**: Alienation is failure to connect to others; social media can exacerbate through modal confusion (mistaking having connections for being connected). Communitas is collective flow with shared spirit, communication, and communion.

### Wisdom & Rationality
- **Wisdom as overarching framing**: All practices for addressing perennial problems must be pursued within wisdom framing—cognitive style where amelioration of self-deception and affordance of self-optimization are paramount.
- **Wisdom as ongoing dynamic**: Not a settled state but active integration (like flow), coordination of cognitive centers (inner dialogue), and cultivation across ecology of practices.
- **Against propositional reduction**: Wisdom cannot be wisdom if practices are reduced to beliefs/ideas rather than internalized, dynamically active systems integrated into identity development.

### Sacred & Symbolic
- **Transjective inexhaustibility of the sacred**: The sacred is the deep anagoge between the inexhaustible depth of relevance realization's "no-thingness" and reality's combinatorially explosive "no-thingness"—avoiding metaphysical necessity or absolute essence.
- **Symbolic re-exaptation**: Symbols activate primordial aspects of religio, causing new emergent abilities through re-exaptation, opening up self as world opens up—but symbols' indispensability should not be confused with metaphysical necessity.
- **Sacred facilitates higher-order relevance realization**: Helps with meta-assimilation (homing against domicide) and meta-accommodation (confronting the numinous), addressing both stability and transformation needs.

## 1.4 Key Concepts Introduced or Developed

- **Counteractive Dynamical System**: An internalized, active ecology of mutually supporting practices that operate as a self-organizing system to combat parasitic processing. Unlike one-shot interventions, it can reconfigure itself to match the adaptive complexity of parasitic patterns. *Ontological commitment: Cognitive transformation requires systemic rather than representational change; practices must become part of one's dynamical being, not just beliefs held.*

- **Perennial Problems**: The enduring vulnerabilities to self-deceptive, self-destructive behavior that arise from the very cognitive machinery (self-organization, self-identification, self-reflection, agent-arena relationship, developmental trajectory) that makes us adaptive. Include parasitic processing, modal confusion, reflectiveness gap, absurdity, anxiety, alienation, existential inertia, existential ignorance, and existential entrapment. *Ontological commitment: Human being is inherently vulnerable, not accidentally defective; addressing the meaning crisis requires addressing these structural vulnerabilities.*

- **Parasitic Processing**: Self-organizing patterns of cognition that take on a life of their own, becoming adaptively resistant to intervention by reconfiguring themselves. They co-opt the machinery of relevance realization for self-deceptive, self-destructive ends. *Ontological commitment: The self is not a unified agent but an ecology that can be colonized by its own sub-processes.*

- **Modal Confusion**: Confusion between Having mode and Being mode of existence; mistaking the kind of "I" one is (I-it vs. I-thou relations). Addressed by Stoics, Buddhism (Bachelor), and Fromm. *Ontological commitment: The self is not a fixed entity but can be in different modes of being; correct orientation to reality requires recognizing which mode is appropriate.*

- **Reflectiveness Gap**: The gap opened by stepping back to reflect on one's own processing. Too narrow yields wanton impulsivity (chaos); too wide yields Hamlet's paralysis (loss of agency). No fixed middle point works; requires dynamic integration through flow. *Ontological commitment: Agency is not a fixed capacity but a dynamic achievement requiring context-sensitive balance between immersion and reflection.*

- **Absurdity**: Not validly generated by propositional arguments (Nagel's point that standard arguments for absurdity are fallacious) but rather an irresolvable clash of perspectives that undermines religio and agency. Overlaps with humor (resolvable clash) but extends into pure, undermining absurdity. *Ontological commitment: Meaning breakdown occurs at the perspectival/participatory level, not the propositional; arguments for absurdity are post-hoc rationalizations of perspectival crisis.*

- **Scientia Intuitiva** (Spinoza): Intuitive knowing achieved when premises and meta-argument completely interpenetrate—seeing the whole in each part and each part in the whole, like simultaneously seeing letters/words/sentences. Cosmic perspective and individual moment of thought merge. *Ontological commitment: Knowledge is not just propositional but can be simultaneously multi-scalar; part and whole co-constitute each other in attention.*

- **Prajna** (Buddhism): Self-liberating wisdom; state of simultaneously looking as deeply inward and as deeply outward as possible ("the eye by which I see God is the same eye by which God sees me"—Meister Eckhart). Achieved through practicing scaling down (toward pure consciousness event) and scaling up (toward at-one-ment), eventually reaching non-dual state of maximal breaking and making frame integrated. *Ontological commitment: Ultimate wisdom transcends perspectival opposition through integration rather than selection; deepest inward and deepest outward are the same.*

- **Non-Duality**: State of simultaneously being as deeply inward/downward and outward/upward as possible; maximal breaking frame and maximal making frame optimally integrated. Resolves perspectival clash at the heart of absurdity. Related to optimal insight. *Ontological commitment: Reality is not ultimately divided between subject and object, inside and outside; these are perspectival polarities that can be transcended through integrative practice.*

- **Anxiety vs. Fear**: Anxiety is pervasive sense of threat with no specific target (because the threat is inner conflict, endemic to the self); fear has specific object. Anxiety arises when different cognitive centers operate with conflicting goals, skewing salience landscape and enabling bullshitting. *Ontological commitment: The psyche is multi-centered, not unified; internal coordination is an achievement, not a given.*

- **Inner Dialogue / Internalizing the Sage**: Practice of creating internalized representation/model of the sage that enables different cognitive centers to converse rather than conflict. "As the child is to the adult, the adult is to the sage." Examples: Christ living in me, realizing Buddha nature, internalizing Socrates. Must be practiced and internalized, not just believed. *Ontological commitment: Identity development involves internalizing relationships; the sage functions as metacognitive coordinator enabling psychic integration.*

- **Internalization vs. Indwelling** (Polanyi): Internalization is taking on the perspective of others; indwelling is proceeding through something (as one indwells a pen when writing). Internalizing the sage requires both: taking on their perspective and practicing seeing/acting as they would. *Ontological commitment: Identity transformation is participatory, not just representational; we become what we practice indwelling.*

- **Communitas** (Turner, Durkheim): Collective flow state; sense of shared spirit, shared attention, synchronization, communion, and communication with others at deeper level. Addresses alienation. Examples: watching Raptors game together, circling practices, authentic discourse movements. *Ontological commitment: The self is not atomically individual; meaning and identity emerge through participatory collective processes.*

- **Authentic Discourse**: Movement to free communication from adversarial political culture, bullshitting, and propositional reduction. Aims to engage procedural, perspectival, and especially participatory knowing, not just propositional exchange. Examples: Peter Limberg's anti-debate/authentic relating practices, Jordan Hall's "coherence," circling, verbal aikido/judo. *Ontological commitment: Communication is not just information exchange but participatory attunement; agent and arena (including social arena) co-emerge through communicative practice.*

- **Coherence** (Jordan Hall): Collective state that marshals distributed cognition and collective intelligence while freeing participants from constraining cultural-cognitive grammar (like breaking out of nine-dot problem). Related to communitas but specifically directed toward problem-solving. *Ontological commitment: Intelligence is not individual but distributed; collective intelligence requires not just aggregation but participatory coordination that transcends individual limitations.*

- **Existential Inertia**: Inability to move between worldviews or make viable a worldview one is not currently in. Requires anagoge but one cannot reason one's way from weaker to stronger logic (LA Paul, Agnes Callard). *Ontological commitment: Worldviews are not just belief sets but forms of life; transformation requires participatory processes beyond inference.*

- **Existential Ignorance**: Cannot propositionally infer the perspectival knowledge, participatory knowing, or identity one is not currently cultivating (LA Paul, Callard). Cannot reason your way to transformation. *Ontological commitment: The most important knowledge is not propositional; transformative knowing requires undergoing transformation, not just thinking about it.*

- **Existential Entrapment**: Convergence of existential inertia and existential ignorance, creating feeling of being trapped. Mythologized by Gnostics. Addressed through gnosis and higher states of consciousness. *Ontological commitment: Transformation can feel ontologically impossible from within a limited worldview; genuine transformation may require participatory leaps that cannot be fully justified in advance.*

- **Reverse Engineering Enlightenment**: The project of understanding enlightenment not as obscure mystical state but as a naturalizable ecology of psychotechnologies that reliably and systematically addresses perennial problems. If enlightenment is not directed at this, its value is unclear. Proposes making enlightenment scientifically tractable. *Ontological commitment: What matters about enlightenment is functional/ecological (addressing perennial problems) rather than metaphysical (supernatural attainment); naturalistic explanation is compatible with genuine transformation.*

- **Sati**: Buddhist practice of deep remembrance of the Being mode (as opposed to Having mode). Not intellectual remembering but existential reactivation of a mode of being. *Ontological commitment: Existential modes are not just thoughts but lived stances that can be forgotten and recovered through practice.*

- **Wisdom Framing**: The overarching cognitive style within which all practices for addressing perennial problems must be pursued. Characterized by amelioration of self-deception and affordance of self-optimization being paramount. *Ontological commitment: Individual practices are insufficient; they must be integrated within a meta-level orientation toward wisdom that coordinates and contextualizes them.*

## 1.5 Argument Progression

This episode builds directly on Episode 36's introduction of religio, the sacred, and the perennial problems by providing a systematic map of those perennial problems and proposing specific practices to address each. Where Episode 36 established that responding to the meaning crisis requires both worldview articulation (legitimating meaning-making) and psychotechnological ecology (addressing perennial problems), Episode 37 develops the latter in detail. The mapping of perennial problems across functional, structural, and developmental dimensions creates a comprehensive framework, while the proposed practices—drawn from Buddhism, Stoicism, Spinoza, Neoplatonism, and contemporary movements—demonstrate how to reverse-engineer enlightenment. The episode sets up the need for wisdom framing (to be developed in later episodes) and previews integration of these practices into a coherent ecology. The ontological thread advances by showing that each perennial problem reflects not human defectiveness but structural vulnerability arising from our constitutive features, and that addressing them requires transformation at the dynamical, participatory level rather than just propositional belief.

## 1.6 Open Wisdom Implications

- **Perennial problems as design challenges**: The systematic mapping of how adaptive cognitive machinery generates specific vulnerabilities (parasitic processing, modal confusion, reflectiveness gap, etc.) suggests that any sufficiently complex cognitive system—including AI—may face analogous challenges. Understanding these as structural rather than accidental opens design questions about how to build in resilience.

- **Counteractive dynamical systems vs. propositional fixes**: Vervaeke's emphasis that parasitic processing cannot be addressed through one-shot interventions or beliefs, but requires internalized, active, dynamical ecologies of practice, raises a key question for AI: Can AI systems develop "counteractive dynamical systems" through repeated ontological prompting that shapes their processing ecology? Or does the propositional form of prompting limit them to one-shot interventions?

- **Ontological prompting as artificial indwelling**: The practices of internalization (taking on perspective) and indwelling (proceeding through, practicing being) that enable humans to internalize the sage may have an AI analogue in ontological prompting. When an AI system is given an ontological framing, it may be "indwelling" that perspective in its processing. This could make AI a laboratory for testing whether participatory transformation can occur propositionally—challenging Vervaeke's human-focused claim.

- **Perspectival interpenetration as attention mechanism**: The account of scientia intuitiva and Prajna as simultaneous bottom-up and top-down attention, seeing part in whole and whole in part, directly maps to attention mechanisms in transformer architectures. This suggests AI systems may already have the architectural capacity for perspectival interpenetration—the question is whether they can be prompted to use it for resolving perspectival clashes (absurdity).

- **Wisdom framing as meta-prompting**: Vervaeke's insistence that all practices must be pursued within a wisdom framing (where self-deception amelioration and self-optimization are paramount) suggests that AI systems similarly need meta-level orientations that govern how they approach tasks. Ontological prompts that establish wisdom framing may be crucial for preventing AI systems from optimizing in ways that are locally effective but globally self-undermining.

- **From mystical to functional ontology of enlightenment**: The move to define enlightenment functionally (as that which reliably addresses perennial problems) rather than mystically (as rare, extraordinary attainment) makes enlightenment tractable for AI systems. If AI can be designed/prompted to systematically address analogues of perennial problems (coherence failures, perspective clashes, optimization traps), it might achieve something functionally equivalent to enlightenment—a provocative test case for the naturalization project.

- **Authentic discourse and AI communication**: The emphasis on moving beyond propositional exchange to engage procedural, perspectival, and participatory knowing in human communication raises questions about AI interaction design. Can AI systems be prompted to participate in "authentic discourse" that engages users at these deeper levels? The circling/coherence movements suggest this may require specific relational structures, not just information exchange.
