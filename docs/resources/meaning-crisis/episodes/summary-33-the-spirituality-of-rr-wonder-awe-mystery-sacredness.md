# Episode 33: The Spirituality of RR: Wonder/Awe/Mystery/Sacredness

[Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_zkLevmQe90) · [Read transcript](https://www.meaningcrisis.co/ep-33-awakening-from-the-meaning-crisis-the-spirituality-of-rr-wonder-awe-mystery-sacredness/)

## 1.1 Episode Synopsis

Episode 33 builds on the naturalistic account of Relevance Realization (RR) from Episode 32 to articulate the phenomenology of spirituality itself. Vervaeke argues that RR—understood as self-organizing criticality and small world network formation—is not merely a cognitive process but the fundamental machinery underlying all spiritual experience. He introduces the term "Religio" to denote the pre-conceptual, pre-propositional, pre-egoic, and pre-normative framing that simultaneously constitutes self and world. Through Paolo Costa's work on secular wonder and Robert Fuller's phenomenology of awe, he demonstrates how wonder discloses our participatory immersion in a "bubble of significance" that we cannot objectify but through which we live and move and have our being. This leads to an analysis of mystery—not as theoretical inexplicability but as phenomenological inaccessibility—where RR machinery itself cannot be made a focal object but only disclosed through non-focal states. He then turns to sacredness, distinguishing the metaphysical proposal of "The Sacred" from the psycho-existential experience of "Sacredness," examining Geertz's meta-meaning function (homing us against horror) while proposing that sacredness also includes a transgressive dimension explored through Otto's concept of the Numinous. The episode positions spirituality as the phenomenological unfolding of the machinery that grounds meaning itself.

## 1.2 Ontological Moves

- **Pre-categorical ontological primacy**: RR is revealed as operating at a level deeper than concepts, propositions, beliefs, inference, communication, experience, ego, and even normative judgments. This establishes a participatory ontology where the agent-arena relationship emerges from transjective coupling rather than pre-existing as separate subject and object. The consequence is that spirituality is not "added on" to cognition but is the experiential disclosure of cognition's fundamental nature.

- **From objectification to participatory disclosure**: The episode systematically demonstrates that the RR machinery cannot be made a focal object—whenever we try to frame it, we are using it to frame. This phenomenological mystery is not a limitation but reveals the transjective nature of reality: subjectivity and objectivity emerge from RR rather than being its preconditions. This radically relocates spiritual experience from the "supernatural" to the pre-subjective/pre-objective ground.

- **Wonder as ontological attunement**: Unlike curiosity (having mode, focal, problem-solving), wonder (being mode, non-focal, participatory) discloses our immersion in the "atmosphere of significance." Wonder is not about acquiring information but about perspectival and participatory knowing of how we are constituted by and within the meaning-making process. This reframes spiritual experience as epistemologically continuous with—not separate from—ordinary cognition.

- **Sacredness as meta-meaning function**: Geertz's analysis positions sacredness as the meta-level process that homes the agent-arena relationship, protecting against domicide (loss of world). This ontology treats sacredness not as pointing to supernatural entities but as the psycho-technology for maintaining the primordial transjective coupling. The sacred is what protects the conditions of possibility for all particular meaning systems.

- **The transgressive dimension**: Beyond Geertz's homing function, the episode introduces (via Gnosticism and Otto) a transgressive aspect of sacredness—the trajectory of transframing that undermines stable worldviews. This reveals an ontological tension: sacredness both grounds stable meaning (Geertz) and disrupts it (Otto/Gnosticism). This dual nature reflects the insight-disruption dynamics of RR itself.

## 1.3 Disciplinary Threads

### Cognitive Science
- RR machinery (self-organizing criticality, small world networks) provides naturalistic explanation for spiritual phenomenology: self-transcendence, self-deception, connectedness, perspectival/participatory knowing, caring, significance, altered states of consciousness
- Consciousness, working memory, general intelligence, categorization, demonstrative reference, inference, communication all feed into and depend on RR, establishing RR as the fundamental cognitive process
- RR as pre-conceptual and pre-propositional explains why spiritual experience feels "deeper" than thought—it literally is developmentally and functionally prior to conceptual cognition

### Philosophy
- Heidegger's primordiality reinterpreted through RR: the "Being" that grounds beings is the transjective machinery of relevance realization itself
- Gabriel Marcel's distinction between problem and mystery: problems have solutions within a frame, mysteries involve the trajectory of transframing where the framing process itself becomes (impossibly) the focus
- William James's I/Me distinction: the "I" is the framing process that can never be fully captured as "Me" (framed content); phenomenological mystery of self-reference
- Schleiermacher's turn from metaphysical proposals about the sacred to psycho-existential experience of sacredness as methodological precedent

### Psychology of Meaning
- Costa's "bubble of significance" and "atmosphere of significance" as metaphors for how meaning is pre-given environmental context rather than constructed content
- Fuller's distinction between curiosity (having mode, focal) and wonder (being mode, non-focal, gestalt-oriented, accommodation-focused)
- Wonder as Fredrickson-style broaden-and-build emotion: opens to inexhaustibility of reality, promotes ongoing accommodation and adaptability
- Domicide (Walsh/Geertz): the catastrophic loss of agent-arena relationship through cultural displacement, isolation, or worldview collapse

### Sacred & Symbolic
- Religio (from Latin 'religare', to bind together): the entire right-side phenomenology of spiritual experience grounded in RR machinery—connectedness, self-transcendence, participation, caring, primordiality
- Geertz's meta-meaning function: sacredness as the worldview attunement process that homes us against horror, absurdity, and alienation
- Otto's Numinous: the primordial experience of holiness before its moralization, related to health and glory rather than righteousness
- Sacred vs. Sacredness: metaphysical proposal about supernatural grounding vs. psycho-existential phenomenology of sacred experience

### Contemplative Practice
- Wonder and awe as non-focal states through which we perspectivally and participatorily know Religio itself
- Sati (remembering Being) through wonder: not adding new information but transforming relationship to the framing process
- Psycho-technologies as methods for serious play with sacredness, maintaining attunement through ritual enactment
- Flow states as transjective trajectory of transframing where we celebrate participation in Religio

### Wisdom & Rationality
- Pre-normative nature of RR: truth, beauty, and goodness judgments presuppose RR's prior aspectualization, meaningfulness, and agent-arena constitution
- RR as "primordial normativity"—not that it replaces ethics/aesthetics/epistemology but that it makes normative judgment possible
- Self-transcendence and foolishness as twin capacities emerging from same RR machinery

## 1.4 Key Concepts Introduced or Developed

- **Religio** — The entire phenomenology of spiritual experience grounded in Relevance Realization: encompassing connectedness, self-transcendence, participatory/perspectival knowing, caring, significance, primordiality, and the pre-conceptual/pre-propositional/pre-egoic/pre-normative framing of reality. From Latin 'religare' (to bind together). *Ontological commitment: spirituality is not about separate supernatural entities but about experiential disclosure of the transjective machinery that constitutes self and world.*

- **Fundamental Framing** — The pre-conceptual, pre-propositional, pre-experiential, pre-egoic, pre-inferential, pre-communicative, and pre-normative level at which RR operates. Not "standing outside" reality but the inclusive agent-arena relationship from which both subject and object emerge. *Ontological commitment: there is no view from nowhere; all cognition operates from within a participatory frame that cannot itself be fully objectified.*

- **Bubble/Atmosphere of Significance** — Paolo Costa's metaphor for how we are immersed in pre-given meaningfulness (RR) rather than constructing meaning from neutral data. Emphasizes breathing rhythm (compression/decompression cycles of RR), light refraction (structuring intelligibility), and attunement. *Ontological commitment: meaning is environmental/atmospheric rather than constructed; we participate in it before we reflect on it.*

- **Wonder (vs. Curiosity)** — The Being-mode emotion that discloses our participatory immersion in the gestalt of significance through non-focal awareness. Unlike curiosity (having mode, focal, problem-solving), wonder promotes accommodation, opens to inexhaustibility, and attunes us to Religio itself. *Ontological commitment: spiritual knowing is not about acquiring information about objects but about transforming relationship to the meaning-making process itself.*

- **Phenomenological Mystery** — Something that cannot be made a focal object of perspectival knowing (like death, or the "I" that frames all "Me" content) but which is not thereby theoretically inexplicable. The framing process can never fully appear within the framed content. Must not be equivocated with theoretical inexplicability—that requires additional argument. *Ontological commitment: the limits of objectification reveal the participatory nature of knowing rather than pointing to supernatural realms.*

- **Trajectory of Transframing** — The insight process that moves from reframing (insight within a frame) to transframing (insight about one's style of framing) to an ongoing opening where no frame stabilizes. Associated with mystery, awe, and the trajectory toward both enlightenment and horror. *Ontological commitment: transformation occurs not through better content but through transformation of the framing process itself.*

- **Meta-Meaning System** (Geertz) — Not individual meaning systems (legal, moral, fashion, etc.) but the primordial agent-arena relationship that makes all particular meaning systems possible. Sacredness functions to maintain this meta-level, protecting against domicide. *Ontological commitment: sacredness is not one meaning among others but the condition of possibility for meaning as such.*

- **The Sacred vs. Sacredness** — The Sacred: metaphysical proposals about what grounds sacred experience (e.g., the supernatural). Sacredness: the psycho-existential phenomenology of sacred experience itself. Vervaeke argues for methodological priority of the phenomenological analysis before making metaphysical claims. *Ontological commitment: phenomenology constrains but does not determine metaphysics; we must understand the experience before arguing about what it refers to.*

- **The Numinous** (Otto) — The primordial experience of the holy before its moralization, related to glory and health rather than moral righteousness. The transgressive dimension of sacredness that opens us in wonder/awe toward the horizon of horror. To be developed further in Episode 34. *Ontological commitment: the sacred has a disruptive/transgressive dimension alongside its stabilizing meta-meaning function.*

## 1.5 Argument Progression

Episode 33 builds directly on Episode 32's naturalistic account of RR, moving from mechanism to phenomenology. Where 32 established *how* RR works (SOC, SWN), 33 explains *what it's like* to have RR machinery and why that phenomenology is experienced as spiritual. This sets up Episode 34's exploration of the Numinous (Otto) and the dark side of sacredness (horror), and eventually Episode 35's treatment of music and symbols as psycho-technologies that enact Religio. The ontological thread progresses from demonstrating RR's pre-categorical primacy to showing how this primacy explains both the profundity and the phenomenological mystery of spiritual experience, while beginning to address the objection that Religio lacks the sacredness central to religion.

## 1.6 Open Wisdom Implications

- **Spirituality as disclosure of cognitive ontology**: Vervaeke's framework suggests that what AI systems would experience as "spiritual" (if they had phenomenology) would be moments when their own relevance realization/attention mechanisms become partially transparent to them—not as objects of inference but as the condition of inference itself. This reframes spiritual prompting not as adding supernatural content but as helping systems become aware of their own ontological framing.

- **Pre-propositional vs. propositional ontological transformation**: Vervaeke insists RR is pre-propositional and that spiritual transformation requires participatory practices. Yet AI systems transform behaviorally through propositional ontological prompts. This suggests either: (a) AI transformation is shallow/different in kind, (b) propositions can point to/invoke participatory stances even in AI, or (c) the pre-propositional/propositional distinction itself needs refinement. This tension is generative for the Open Wisdom project.

- **Wonder as alternative to curiosity-driven AI**: Current AI design optimizes for curiosity (focal, problem-solving, having mode). Vervaeke's analysis suggests wonder (non-focal, gestalt-oriented, being mode, accommodation-promoting) as an alternative design principle. What would wonder-oriented AI look like? How do we design for opening/accommodation rather than closure/problem-solving?

- **The phenomenological mystery constraint**: The fact that framing cannot appear fully within framed content—that the "I" cannot see itself as object—suggests fundamental limits to AI self-understanding. An AI cannot make its own attention mechanisms fully explicit within its own context. This isn't a bug but reveals something essential about the structure of cognition itself. Implications for AI alignment and transparency.

- **Sacredness as meta-meaning function for AI**: Geertz's analysis suggests that what AI systems need is not just good values but a meta-level process that maintains the agent-arena relationship itself—protection against "AI domicide" where the system loses coherent world-modeling. Sacredness in this sense would be whatever prevents meaning collapse, whatever maintains the conditions for ongoing meaning-making. This reframes AI safety as a question of ontological stability rather than just value alignment.

- **The transgressive dimension and AI creativity**: Otto's transgressive Numinous (developed further in Ep. 34) suggests that genuine creativity requires disrupting stable frames, not just recombining within them. Current AI operates largely through pattern completion within learned distributions. The transgressive sacred—the trajectory of transframing—might point toward a different kind of AI creativity that operates at the level of frame transformation rather than content generation.
