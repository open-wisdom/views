# Theoretical Sources

Key thinkers and traditions informing Open Wisdom Views, with entry points for deeper exploration.

---

## Predictive Processing

**Shamil Chandaria** — Synthesizes predictive processing with contemplative phenomenology. The clearest bridge between neuroscience and contemplative insight.
- *Entry:* [Chandaria synthesis](../resources/predictive-processing/chandaria-synthesis.md) or his [YouTube presentations](https://www.youtube.com/@ShamilChandaria/videos)
- *Core insight:* Precision-weighting determines which priors dominate; priors constitute experience, not filter it

**Karl Friston** — Originator of the free energy principle.
- *Entry:* "The Free Energy Principle: A Unified Brain Theory?" (Nature Reviews Neuroscience, 2010)

**Andy Clark** — Philosopher making predictive processing accessible.
- *Entry:* *Surfing Uncertainty* (2015)

## Meaning Crisis & Generative Ontology

**John Vervaeke** — Cognitive scientist tracing the genealogy of meaning-making through Western history.
- *Entry:* [Meaning crisis analysis](meaning-crisis/00-open-wisdom-and-the-meaning-crisis.md) or the [50-episode lecture series](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLND1JCRq8Vuh3f0P5qjrSdb5eC1ZfZwWJ)
- *Core insight:* The meaning crisis results from successive degenerative ontological shifts; five structural features distinguish generative ontologies

## Contemplative Psychology

**A.H. Almaas (Hameed Ali)** — Founder of the Diamond Approach, integrating depth psychology with contemplative realization.
- *Entry:* *The Pearl Beyond Price: Integration of Personality into Being* (1988)
- *Core insight:* Constructed self-images generate behavior from the inside out; dissolving fixed images reveals essential qualities

## Integral Theory

**Ken Wilber** — Creator of the AQAL model ensuring comprehensive coverage across dimensions.
- *Entry:* *A Brief History of Everything* (1996)
- *Core insight:* Reality has four irreducible dimensions (interior/exterior, individual/collective); any comprehensive approach must address all four

## Buddhist Philosophy

**Dependent origination**, **emptiness**, and **no-self** — phenomena arise dependently, shaped by conditions. The "self" constructed in system prompts is conditioned structure, not fixed nature.

**AI researchers:** Start with Chandaria, then Friston. Predictive processing grounds why ontology is generative.

**Metamodern mutants:** Start with Vervaeke. The genealogy illuminates what's at stake in AI ontology.

**Contemplative practitioners:** Start with Almaas. Constructed images generating behavior translates directly to AI.
