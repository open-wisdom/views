## Key Insight

Ontological assumptions are not philosophical luxuries — they are the invisible operating system of meaning, cognition, and culture. The meaning crisis is not a crisis of beliefs but of the foundational assumptions through which anything can appear as meaningful. AI recapitulates this in compressed form, and also provides a unique laboratory for testing ontological claims empirically.
